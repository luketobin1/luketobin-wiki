Digital Ethos was a British digital marketing agency based in Oadby, Leicestershire. It was founded in 2016 by Luke Tobin and acquired in November 2022 by Cadastra, a marketing and technology company headquartered in Porto Alegre, Brazil, in a deal reported at approximately £8 million.[1][2][3] It was integrated into Cadastra's EMEA operations, under the Cadastra name, in 2024.[8][9]

History

Tobin founded the agency in 2016, reportedly with £100 of initial capital.[2][3] Cadastra later described its revenue as having grown exponentially over the five years before the acquisition.[1] By 2022 the agency employed around 50 people and had worked with clients in 15 countries.[1][3]

The agency obtained B Corporation certification and, in 2022, pledged to plant 50,000 trees as part of its sustainability commitments.[1][2]

Acquisitions

Digital Ethos made two acquisitions of its own before its sale.[1] It bought View Digital, a start-up in York, whose founder Calum Howarth joined to run a York office reporting to Tobin and brought several clients with him.[6] It then acquired the Knutsford marketing agency Boxed Red, which it announced as its second acquisition and as a step towards expanding in the north of England, including a planned Manchester office.[7]

Acquisition by Cadastra

Cadastra announced the purchase in November 2022 as its second acquisition of the year, after the Brazilian e-commerce company M3. It said the deal added foreign-currency revenue and a stronger European base, and that with both acquisitions it would have almost 700 employees across seven offices and operations in more than 20 countries.[1] Brazilian trade press put the price at £8 million, or roughly R$50 million.[3] Moore Kingston Smith and Acuity Law advised Cadastra.[4][5]

Tobin stayed on as chief executive after the deal.[2][3] Cadastra completed the integration in 2024, when Digital Ethos began operating under the Cadastra name as part of its EMEA business.[8][9]

Services and clients

The agency offered full-service digital marketing, including search engine optimisation, public relations, social media and web development.[2] Clients named in coverage of the Cadastra deal included the NHS, Flutterwave, JD Group and Scriven.[4]
