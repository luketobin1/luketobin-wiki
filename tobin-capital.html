Tobin Capital is the private investment vehicle of the British entrepreneur Luke Tobin. It takes minority stakes in private companies and works with their management teams on sales and operations.[1][2] CB Insights records it as founded in 2020 and based in Leicester.[2]

History[edit]

Tobin developed Tobin Capital alongside Digital Ethos, and said at the time of the agency's 2022 sale to Cadastra that he would continue building it in parallel.[1] His publisher describes its focus as future-facing ventures in areas including female-led brands, space, sustainability, deep technology and biotechnology.[3]

Investments[edit]

CB Insights lists eight investments made between October 2021 and May 2023, across seed, Series A and private equity rounds. Among those it names is a private equity deal in Sook in May 2023.[2]
